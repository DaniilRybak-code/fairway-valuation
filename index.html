Before you ask

Is this a real valuation?

No. It is a set of methods, the metrics behind them and a written rationale, which is the thing you argue from. It is not a 409A, a fairness opinion, or a regulated valuation.

Why is there no single number at the top?

Because we would have to invent it. A headline range that is not derived from the methods below is a guess with a font size. The methods are the evidence, and a banker's read on where inside them you sit is what comes back by email.

Do you adjust my growth rate?

No. You give us two numbers, what you grew last year and what you plan for next year, and the forward figures are built from your plan exactly as you gave it. There is no haircut and no coefficient of ours anywhere in the arithmetic. That also means the plan has to survive the meeting on your evidence, which is the conversation the reviewer will have with you.

Where do your numbers come from?

Tap any reference metric on the field and it tells you, with the publication and the date. Listed sector multiples come from Damodaran at NYU Stern, stage medians from Carta, exchange rates from the ECB. Anything we cannot source is drawn locked rather than filled in with something plausible.

Why are some rows marked in build?

Because they are. Those rows will be priced from a live set of comparable companies chosen for your business, with every peer named on hover. Until that is wired up they show the metric they will price rather than a number we made up. We would rather tell you than fill the gap.

Who sees my numbers?

Only the reviewer. Your inputs, notes and any link you share are not sold, shared, or shown to investors. The service that writes the commentary receives the business figures with your name, email and phone stripped out, so it never sees who you are. The investor list is one-way, from us to you.

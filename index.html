Before you ask

Is this a real valuation?

No. It's an informational range and a written rationale — the thing you argue from. It isn't a 409A, a fairness opinion, or a regulated valuation.

What does "banker-reviewed" actually mean here?

A person with investment banking coverage experience reads your inputs and the range before anything is sent to you — on the free check as well as the paid report. It is not a model output with a badge on it.

Why is the free range so wide?

Because six multiple-choice answers can't see your margins, retention or unit economics. Narrowing it is exactly what the review and the report do.

Who sees my numbers?

Only the reviewer. Your inputs, notes and any link you share aren't sold, shared, or shown to investors — the investor list is one-way, from us to you.

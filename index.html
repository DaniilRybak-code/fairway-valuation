Before you ask

Is this a real valuation?

No. It is an informational range and a written rationale, the thing you argue from. It is not a 409A, a fairness opinion, or a regulated valuation.

What does banker-reviewed actually mean here?

A person with bulge bracket investment banking experience reads your inputs and the range before anything is sent to you, on the free check as well as the paid report.

Why is the free range so wide?

Because nine multiple-choice answers cannot see your margins, retention or unit economics. Narrowing it is exactly what the review and the report do.

Who sees my numbers?

Only the reviewer. Your inputs, notes and any link you share are not sold, shared, or shown to investors. The investor list is one-way, from us to you.

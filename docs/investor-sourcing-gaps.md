# What the call list cannot cover, 3-Sep-2026

The founder-facing list is headed "writing first cheques in your sector right now". A house
reaches it only with a dated deal, a first-cheque figure, a geography and a sector in our own
archetype vocabulary. This file is what we still cannot answer, so the next pull is aimed rather
than general.

## Where we are

| | |
|---|---|
| houses in the table | 445 |
| carry a CALLABLE layer | 92 |
| can actually render to a founder | 75 |
| test founders getting fewer than three houses | 2 of 43 |

The 92 is down from 140 and that is the enrichment working, not a loss: 47 houses were moved to
the EVIDENCE layer because a human read each one against the heading and said no. Coatue,
BlackRock, Baillie Gifford, Benchmark, T. Rowe Price and 42 others were on a first-cheque list
because an automated rule promoted anything with a deal inside twelve months. A seed founder told
to call Benchmark is worse served than one shown six houses that write their cheque.

## Gap 1: seventeen callable houses cannot render

Nine are blocked on a cheque figure, six on both a cheque and a geography, two on geography
alone. In every case the researcher looked and found nothing published, which is a real answer
and is recorded as `NOT STATED` rather than guessed.

**No cheque figure published anywhere on their own site.** Atomico, Battery Ventures, Bessemer,
Greycroft, HOF Capital, Index Ventures, Insight Partners, Lightspeed, Ribbit.

**No cheque and no investing geography.** Amplify, Bain Capital Ventures, Benchmark, Founders
Fund, Thrive Capital, and one more.

**Geography only.** Acrew Capital, Freestyle VC.

These are not a research failure. benchmark.com carries two office addresses and no investment
criteria; thrivecap.com is one sentence. The options are to leave them off the call list, which
is what we do now, or to record an observed cheque band from their own announced rounds and label
it as observed rather than stated. **That is a decision for Daniil and it is not made.**

## Gap 2: four archetypes have no fund at all

The seed screen has eight coarse sectors. These founder archetypes fall outside all of them, so
the two fixtures tagged the first one get an empty list:

- **Consumer & Prosumer Software** (2 of 43 fixtures). A consumer app is not a D2C brand and
  mapping it to "consumer brands and D2C" would be inventing coverage.
- **Online Learning.** There is no edtech sector in the screen.
- **Market Infrastructure & Exchange** and **Financial Data & Index** now reach the fintech
  infrastructure funds, which is a fair translation, but no fund names either directly.

**The pull that closes this:** seed and Series A funds that state a cheque range and publish
recent dated deals in consumer software / consumer subscription apps, and in edtech. Ten to
fifteen of each would take the two remaining empty fixtures to a full list.

## Gap 3: the quiz never asks where the founder is

Every one of the 43 fixtures carries `country=None`, because no fork asks it. The matcher now
treats an unknown country as unknown rather than as a failed geography test, and labels those
cards "we have not asked where you are based" so nobody reads "exact fit" and assumes we checked.
But geography is one of three facets and we are throwing it away on every founder. A UK pre-seed
founder and a US one should not get the same list.

**One question, on every fork.** It is the cheapest accuracy in the whole product.

## Gap 4: three published cheque figures are years old

Freestyle VC's is a TechCrunch line dated 31 Mar 2022, Square Peg's a blog post dated 21 Nov 2022,
Sequoia's the Jan 2023 Arc announcement. Each is the only figure that house publishes anywhere.
They now render with the date on the card: "First cheque $1.5m to $3m (the only figure they
publish, dated 31 Mar 2022)". Better than hiding them and better than presenting them as current.

## Gap 5: Greycroft, and what we refused to use

A $500K to $50m range for Greycroft circulates on aggregator profiles and appears on no
first-party page. It is not in the file. Recording it would have made Greycroft renderable and
put a range in front of a founder that Greycroft has never published. Left out on purpose.

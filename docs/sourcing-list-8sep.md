word ("pass it now").** Where a fixture already holds a near-perfect comparable, look up that
company's own neighbours online (its competitors' pages, its press, who its investors also backed)
and bring the priced ones in. Daniil's example: Mambu led to Thought Machine. The pass started from
the 91 DIRECT names across 55 fixtures and produced **200 candidates with a source each (46 listed,
154 private), none loaded, no figure written**: `docs/a16-neighbour-pass-9sep.md`, with the counts
in and out and the caveats. Reading each source for the figure is the load, and it is step (1) below.
